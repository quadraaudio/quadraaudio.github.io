    Logic Pro

    Sound thinking.

    Logic Pro is the ultimate music creation experience for Mac and iPad. It features an extensive collection of instruments and effects. And it helps you produce and edit audio with intelligent tools for beat-making, songwriting, and remixing.

    Apple Creator StudioCreator Studio A suite deal for any creator.

    Logic Pro is part of Apple Creator Studio, the essential collection of creative apps that includes Final Cut Pro, Pixelmator Pro, and more.

    Beat‑Making Tools

    Unleash the beats.

    Build the perfect drum kit, program drums and bass lines, and transform samples into entirely new instruments. You can even reshape melodies and rhythms with ease. It’s everything you need to create beats, shape sounds, and bring your ideas to life.

    • Build custom drum kits.

      Drum Machine Designer lets you add samples and unique plug‑ins to each drum pad. Integrated with Step Sequencer, it makes building beats fast and intuitive.

    • Quick Sampler.

      Record, chop, flip, and transform samples into entirely new playable instruments.

    • Drum Synth.

      Create synthesized drum sounds with this powerful but easy-to-use plug-in. Integrated into Drum Machine Designer, Drum Synth gives you a focused set of sound-shaping tools.

    • Step Sequencer.

      Inspired by the programming styles of vintage drum machines, Step Sequencer makes building beats out of drums, bass, and melodies remarkably simple. You can even add variation with Note Repeat and randomized step playback.

    Sounds, Instruments, and Effects

    Sounds by the trackload.

    Logic Pro fuels your creativity with loads of sounds, instruments, and effects from a wide range of styles and genres. Tap into a huge content library, incredible synths, and a mind-blowing array of effects to create the sounds you’ve only imagined.

    • Sound Library.

      Find inspiration with an expanding collection of instruments, loops, and samples, including Producer Packs created by hitmakers like Oak Felder and Boys Noize.

    • Instruments.

      From history-defining basses and classic keyboards to modern synths and meticulously sampled pianos, you get studio-quality sounds built for today’s music.

    • Effects.

      Shape your track’s sonic character with industry-leading reverbs, delays, modulation effects, EQs, and compressors.

    • Third‑party plug‑ins.

      Access tens of thousands of instruments and effects made by today’s leading developers.2

    Plug‑ins and play.

    Logic Pro for Mac gives you access to a massive library of plug-ins and sounds, along with a growing collection of royalty-free sounds, loops, and instruments. Choose from Apple-designed Sound Packs and Producer Packs from today’s leading artists. Each pack includes hundreds of loops, samples, patches, and more.

    Plug-Ins and Sounds

    Ear candy. Choose your flavors.

    Logic Pro gives you a massive collection of plug-ins and sounds to bring any musical idea to life. Quickly build sampled instruments with drag-and-drop workflows. Add color to your tracks with meticulously designed models of coveted analog hardware. And then finish your mix with intelligent mastering.

    Instruments

    From vintage to visionary.

    Create amazing sounds using authentic re-creations of vintage equipment, powerful modern synthesizers, and complex multisampled instruments.

    Resynthesize any sample into a unique playable instrument. Set up to four playback points and explore modes that offer different ways to manipulate sounds. Try with your own sample or start from a library of designed sounds.

    Quickly turn any sample into a playable instrument. Flex Time keeps your sample in time with your song no matter which notes you play. And with Slice Mode you can create vocal chops or flip a drum loop to make it your own.

    Drag and drop samples to create custom kits, layer sounds, and apply effects to each pad. Quick Sampler and Drum Synth controls are directly integrated into the interface so you can quickly edit sounds. Then use Step Sequencer to drive Drum Machine Designer to create the perfect beat.

    With a library of over 3000 sounds, Alchemy is the ultimate sample-manipulation synth. It has powerful morphing and resynthesis tools, multiple sound-generating engines, and a vast range of filters, modulation routes, arpeggiation options, and effects for limitless tones and textures.

    Re-create classic ’70s and ’80s sounds with a wide-ranging set of vintage synths, each with intuitive controls. Perfect for everything from analog basses and sweeping pads to bells and brash leads.

    Choose from six iconic bass guitars. Customize the sound with a variety of tone controls, including scrapes and string noise.

    Select from three meticulously sampled pianos and customize them with a mix of multiple mic positions, pedal and key noise, release samples, and sympathetic resonance.

    Effects

    Shape your sound.

    Use the vast collection of plug-ins to help sculpt and perfect the color and clarity of your tracks.

    Applies intelligent, professional-grade enhancements to deliver release-ready sound. Automatically adjusts dynamics and fine-tunes frequencies, so your mix sounds great whether on the dance floor or through headphones.

    Slice, rearrange, and add pitch-shifting effects to your tracks. In the studio or on stage, you can tweak speed and pitch, adjust volume, and add repeats to any slice.

    Add DJ-style effects like stutters, echoes, filters, reverse playback, and vinyl scratches and stops. And with Logic Remote on your iPhone or iPad, you can use Multi-Touch to control up to seven effects at once.

    A high-end reverb plug-in that uses unique modeling to reproduce natural and otherworldly spaces. ChromaVerb features 14 models with tempo-synced parameters, up to 100 seconds of decay, and a visual spectrum that shows how the reverb complements your tracks.

    The Vintage EQ collection brings authentic re-creations of three of the most revered analog hardware EQs, with support for mono and stereo and control over analog heat.

    Add rhythmic movement to any track by arranging filters and effects, all driven by three independent step sequencers. Eight effects can be organized in any order to customize diverse sound-design options.

    Bring rich analog color and character to your tracks with the feel of tubes, tape, and vintage hardware.

    Sound Library

    Inspiration on tap.

    Explore an ever-expanding Sound Library filled with free, royalty-free premium Sound Packs. Preview and install your favorites, watch Producer Pack videos for inspiration, and easily manage storage by removing packs you no longer need. And you can be reminded when new packs are available, so you’re always tuned in.

    Intelligence

    Brilliance on board.

    Packed with AI features that build on Apple Intelligence and powered by Apple silicon, Logic Pro gives you intuitive ways to make beats, write songs, remix tracks, and jam with Session Players.3 It’s your creative collaborator in all things musical.

    • Split audio into isolated tracks.

      Stem Splitter separates drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano, and other instruments, so it’s easy to apply effects, sample, or change the mix.

    • Mastering Assistant.

      Make your mix sound great on any device. Mastering Assistant listens to your track and transforms it using analog mastering magic that you can adjust to get your project ready for release.

    • Instant chord detection.

      Use Chord ID to quickly identify chords from any part of your song and Session Players will follow along. Chord ID pulls the chords from MIDI and audio regions and drops them in the Chord Track.

    • Pitch Correction.

      Logic Pro listens to pitch using machine learning and makes adjustments in real time. The result? A perfectly in-tune, natural‑sounding vocal.

    • Smart Tempo.

      Logic Pro automatically takes any audio and matches it to the tempo of your project. Or you can record without a metronome and Logic Pro will follow the tempo of your performance.

    • Get that analog sound.

      ChromaGlow adds the texture of tubes, tape, and other classic analog hardware to bring more depth and color to your tracks.

    Session Players vibe with you.

    Call on a group of AI-powered musicians at a moment’s notice. Session Players take your direction on the fly and lay down polished synth, bass, keyboard, and drum grooves that perfectly fit your song.

    Create and edit chord progressions in the Chord Track and your Session Players will follow along.

    Add stylish synth and heavy bass as the synth player responds instantly to your feedback and follows your chords. Then take full control of your sound in real time, even with third-party plug-ins or external synthesizers.

    Lay down realistic keyboard parts on your songs. Direct the player’s complexity, intensity, and range or go deeper into feel, voicing, and movement.

    Choose from a range of studio-quality electric bass guitars — from fingerstyle and picked to muted and slap — with techniques like hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, and harmonics.

    Advanced microsampling delivers natural acoustic bass tones with slides and dynamics, all customizable with EQ, compression, and reverb.

    Play realistic acoustic drum parts using flexible styles, dynamics, and fills with professionally mixed and customizable drum kits.

    Create beats in styles from EDM to hip-hop, and mix and match your favorite drum kits from a massive, ever-growing collection of kit pieces.

    Workflows

    Move at the speed of sound.

    From recording and editing to mixing and mastering, the workflow features in Logic Pro accelerate every part of your music‑making.

    • Flashback Capture.

      Now when you accidentally come up with something brilliant but weren’t recording, Flashback Capture has your back. This temporary buffer is always on, capturing audio and MIDI in the background.

    • Quick Swipe Comping.

      Create the perfect performance from multiple takes more easily than you ever imagined. You can even save multiple comps and quickly switch between them.

    • Time and pitch perfection.

      Use Flex Time to warp the timing to your taste, and Flex Pitch to fix that almost-perfect take so you don’t have to re‑record.

    • Live Loops.

      Explore musical ideas using a grid of loops and phrases you can trigger and experiment with unique arrangements on the fly.

    • Get your groove.

      Easily record and edit MIDI performances. Transform a loose performance into one that locks into the groove while preserving the original feel.

    Take it from studio to MainStage.

    MainStage turns your Mac into an instrument, voice processor, or guitar rig. So the sound you love on your recording can be the sound your audience hears. Setup is fast, teardown is faster, and everything in between is more reliable.

    MainStage

    Sound as great on stage as you do in the studio.

    Love your studio sound? Take your Mac on stage and you’ll have all your favorite Logic Pro and third-party plug-ins with you. So you can deliver a performance to remember.

    Custom Setups

    The right rig for every kind of performer.

    Whether your instrument is a keyboard, a guitar, or your voice, MainStage gives you the tools you need to sound your absolute best.

    Bring the authentic sounds of history’s greatest keyboards or state-of-the-art synths to the stage. And take your sounds even further on a keyboard with Arpeggiator, Chord Trigger, and the magic of Floating Split Points.

    Play live with the great tones you’ve created using Amp Designer, Bass Amp Designer, Pedalboard, and Audio Units effects. You also get a tuner, foot-controlled tap tempo, and MIDI foot controller support.

    Keep your hands free by using foot pedals to control your vocal effects. And display multipage PDFs with lyrics, chord changes, and notation on your screen — so you can focus on singing.

    Your Studio Sound

    The total performance package.

    Take your songs straight to the stage with the same instruments, Sound Library, and Smart Controls you use in Logic Pro. With Mac at the core of your live rig, you can create the set you want.

    Use your favorite hardware. MainStage supports your love of knobs, buttons, and faders. And the simple yet powerful interface works with any MIDI device. Just assign a physical control once and it stays mapped until you change it. Support for Ableton Link makes it easy to stay in sync with your favorite hardware and fellow musicians.

    Performing

    Follow the music from the studio to the stage.

    Customize your layout and focus on only what you need for a smooth, clutter‑free performance.

    Customize your settings and control your sound. Drag and drop controls into your MainStage layout, then customize their look, function, and placement. Alignment guides, Lift and Stamp, and adaptive Smart Controls make setup fast.

    Mixing

    Level up your mix.

    Logic Pro offers a full-featured mixer that lets you take control of even the most demanding mix to create polished, release‑ready tracks.

    • Logic Pro Mixer.

      Bring the different parts of your song into focus using the powerful mixer. And shape your sound with a complete suite of professional plug‑ins.

    • Control signal flow.

      Group, route, and send signals wherever you want. Use Track Stacks to organize tracks, and make your mixes come alive with dynamic automation.

    • Spatial Audio.

      Create fully immersive Spatial Audio mixes using integrated Dolby Atmos tools, then export them as industry-standard ADM files ready to submit for Apple Music and other streaming services.4

    iPad

    Work anywhere.
    Without missing a beat.

    Logic Pro for iPad puts a professional music studio right at your fingertips. Intelligent features and an intuitive interface made specifically for iPad give you what you need to make incredible music wherever you are.

    Intelligent awareness of the massive Logic Pro Sound Library helps you find loops and sounds fast with natural language search or discover similar ones with a simple drag and drop. No tags, guesses, or filters required.

    Play Surfaces provide expressive control over software instruments, all with the power of touch. Tap piano keys, drum pads, chord strips — even swipe to strum guitars — to turn your iPad into all kinds of instruments.

    Transform your tracks by drawing detailed automation and making precise edits using Apple Pencil.5

    Easily move your projects between Mac and iPad to create, refine, and release your work — in the studio or on the road.

    Comp anywhere you go using touch or Apple Pencil and piece together the perfect performance from your favorite takes.

    Music Creation with Apple

    Everything in perfect harmony.

    Logic Pro on Apple silicon effortlessly runs demanding plug-ins and large projects at the highest fidelity with flow and finesse. It also works in concert with the rest of Apple Creator Studio, Apple devices, and other apps.

    Vocalists can layer a vocal track on top of an existing instrumental recording — without the need for headphones.6 Then Logic Pro can import a layered file and automatically create vocal and instrumental tracks that you can develop into a final song.

    Control Logic Pro on your Mac with Multi-Touch gestures using iPhone or iPad. Play software instruments, mix tracks, and control features like Live Loops and Remix FX — from anywhere in the room.

    Open a GarageBand project in Logic Pro and tap into the power of built-in editing tools and plug-ins, so you can take your songs even further.
