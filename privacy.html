Quadra Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

1. Privacy Fundamental Principles

At Quadra, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. All audio processing, kernel process taps, and matrix routings performed by Hydra take place strictly on your local macOS machine. No audio content, waveform data, or microphone signals are ever uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any external server.

2. Data We Collect for Quadra ID

When you authenticate using Quadra ID (via Google OAuth2 or email verification), we collect only your primary account email address and hardware identifier (machineID) strictly for verifying commercial software entitlements and issuing Ed25519 cryptographic license signatures.

3. Payments & Checkout

Purchases processed via PayPal Checkout SDK on quadraaudio.com are handled directly by PayPal. Quadra does not store or process credit card numbers or sensitive banking details.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your Quadra ID data, please contact legal@quadraaudio.com.
